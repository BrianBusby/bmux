# Trustworthy session and turn visibility

Status: baseline investigation started 2026-11-13 on `session-visibility-baseline`, based on `origin/main` at `73994ec15`. The six dependent slices are registered in Project Truth. The baseline slice is active. PR #227 merged, and its caller-retrieval acceptance remains under observation; its unresolved caveats are preserved. No slice is accepted by this record.

## Intent and boundaries

Make ordinary Codex sessions understandable without opening transcripts: objective, meaningful work, concurrent operations, developments, next action, attention, and observation health. A turn is not a work boundary or proof of task completion. Use PE's canonical evidence, factual outcomes and SessionWorkModel, with bmux responsible for provider observation and presentation. Keep explicit agent reports, observed execution and inference distinguishable and evidence-linked. Do not join identities by time or text alone, infer stalls from silence, or let commentary clear unresolved attention. Preserve privacy and existing retention policy. This initiative does not build an orchestrator, messaging broker or knowledge compiler.

## Dependencies and gates

1. **Baseline:** trace actual ordinary CLI signals through source, ingestion, PE and native rendering; classify losses; produce representative fixtures and receipt/visible latency evidence; fix confirmed defects. Verify a real plan and status updates or identify the precise unsupported boundary. Include interruption, resume, input, concurrent operations and silence. Do not treat the limited probe below as that gate.
2. **Summary:** useful evidence-linked objective, work, concurrent activity, progress, attention and observation health in a primary surface, including unplanned sessions and restart/reconnect reconciliation.
3. **Progress:** preserve discoveries, changes and validation separately, including corrections and stale validation; compare any bounded asynchronous synthesis with a simpler baseline and prevent stale writes.
4. **Plans and reporting:** preserve explicit plan snapshots and revision uncertainty; evaluate guidance in ordinary and resumed sessions without mandatory plans for trivial work.
5. **Scanning:** validate hierarchy, accessibility and comprehension across five live sessions, including attention and observation loss.
6. **Reliability and contract:** replay and native integration acceptance, performance and cross-surface consistency; expose stable identities, relationships, evidence, revisions, capabilities, freshness and recovery ordering for future coordination.

Each gate depends on its predecessor. Existing `live_terminal_codex_evidence_ingestion`, `coding_agent_evidence_source_reconciliation`, `deterministic_turn_outcome_projection`, `session_work_model_contract_foundation`, `milestone_inference` and `blocker_approach_change_semantics` are prerequisites or reuse points, not new parallel pipelines. PE owns semantic and durable state; raw provider streams remain ephemeral under current policy. Proposed targets are p95 receipt-to-visible activity and attention within two seconds, with source-to-receipt measured separately, zero unsupported completion/cleared attention/cross-session contamination/stale overwrites, and five-session performance and reviewer comprehension measured. Change targets only with a recorded rationale before implementation.

## Newly identified prerequisite: revision integrity

Independent source review found that PE factual snapshots span multiple SQL reads, semantic publication reads and supersedes separately, and session revision uses the maximum event sequence scoped to an optional envelope session ID while projection updates use payload ownership. A payload may affect multiple sessions or move ownership. The factual turn read also joins child facts by turn ID without checking their session against the owning turn, so mismatched ownership requires explicit reconciliation rather than an assumed association. Therefore an older interpretation can replace a newer one, and comparing the existing revision alone cannot establish a coherent snapshot. These are source-level findings; a live failure has not been claimed.

The additional `session_visibility_revision_integrity` prerequisite is registered between baseline and useful-summary delivery. Its implementation must resolve inconsistent child ownership, account for prior and new direct and referenced-turn owners and replay/backfill, provide coherent cross-connection reads, and use opt-in atomic conditional publication with conflict and idempotence semantics. Preserve existing producers and historical evidence. Exercise concurrent publishers, append during reading, same-payload newer evidence, and missing/mismatched ownership. Until resolved, affected current claims must be treated conservatively; the local completed-command fix in branch `session-activity-truth` is not a freshness guarantee.

A bounded parallel correction for already answered input requests is in local branch `session-attention-resolution`, with red/green regression and focused CLI tests. It handles matching call and turn context; it does not yet establish full attention lifecycle or live native behavior. Neither local branch is merged or an accepted slice.
